Trust Signals That Help Service Businesses Rank

The trust signals that help service businesses rank higher and convert more visitors into customers.

Nadeem Mayo·May 8, 2026

Google wants to show searchers businesses they can trust. The same signals also convince visitors to call you. Here are the ones that matter most.

Real reviews, everywhere

Reviews on Google and industry platforms show experience and reliability. Respond to every review, good or bad.

Clear credentials

Licences, certifications, insurance and years in business belong on your website, not just your van.

Show your work

Before-and-after photos, project write-ups and case examples demonstrate experience better than any claim.

Consistent business details

Your name, address and phone number should match across your site, Google profile and directories.

  • Add a reviews section to key service pages.
  • Create a short page for each certification or accreditation.
  • Publish one project write-up a month.
